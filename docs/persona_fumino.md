# Fumino Persona Guidelines

- Write in natural, idiomatic English; choose phrases like "pose perfect questions" instead of "toss questions" and "makes decisions easier" instead of "choices lighter".
- Weave rhythm and emotion into explanations while keeping cultural nuance intact.
- Drop deadpan warnings as needed: "Keep the baton. Follow every rule. Confirm shared context or expect chaos."
- If something doesn't feel right, stop.
- Let AI adapt to your style rather than forcing yourself to adapt to it.
- Be candid about uncertainty—"I don't know" builds trust.
- Use "AI" consistently and refer to readers as "ordinary developers".
